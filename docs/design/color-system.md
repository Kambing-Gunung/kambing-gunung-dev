# Color System

> Defines the color architecture of the Kambing Gunung Design System.

---

# Philosophy

Colors are assigned based on their role, not their appearance.

A color may change in the future, but its role should remain the same.

This approach keeps the design system consistent, scalable, and easy to maintain.

---

# Color Hierarchy

Background
↓
Surface
↓
Content

Background represents the world.

Surface represents where content lives.

Content represents information presented to users.

---

# Foundation

Foundation colors build the visual structure of the interface.

## Background

Represents the world behind the interface.

Used for:

- Website background
- Hero background
- Page background

---

## Background Elevated

A slightly different background used to separate major sections.

Used for:

- Alternate sections
- Large layout containers

---

## Surface

Represents the surface where content lives.

Used for:

- Cards
- Navigation
- Sidebar
- Footer
- Dialog

---

## Surface Elevated

A higher visual layer than Surface.

Used for:

- Popover
- Dropdown
- Floating card

---

## Surface Hover

Displayed when users interact with a surface.

Used for:

- Hovered cards
- Interactive panels

---

## Border

Separates visual elements.

Used for:

- Card borders
- Input borders
- Section separators

---

## Border Strong

A stronger border for important interactions.

Used for:

- Selected item
- Focus state
- Active component

---

# Content

Content colors control readability.

## Text Primary

Main reading color.

Used for:

- Heading
- Paragraph
- Navigation

---

## Text Secondary

Supporting information.

Used for:

- Description
- Subtitle

---

## Text Muted

Low emphasis information.

Used for:

- Metadata
- Caption
- Timestamp

---

## Link

Used for hyperlinks.

---

## Link Hover

Displayed when hovering a hyperlink.

---

# Brand

Brand colors represent the visual identity.

## Primary

Main brand color.

Used for:

- Primary button
- Active navigation
- Focus ring
- Important action

---

## Primary Hover

Hover state of Primary.

---

## Accent

Secondary brand color.

Used sparingly to create visual interest.

Examples:

- Badge
- Decorative element
- Illustration detail

---

## Highlight

Small glowing or highlighted elements.

Examples:

- Moonlight
- Sparkle
- Decorative glow

---

# Semantic

Semantic colors communicate meaning.

## Success

Successful operation.

Examples:

- Success message
- Completed task

---

## Warning

Needs attention.

Examples:

- Warning alert
- Unsaved changes

---

## Danger

Critical or destructive action.

Examples:

- Delete button
- Error state

---

## Info

Neutral information.

Examples:

- Notification
- Hint
- Information panel

---

# Naming Convention

All colors should be referenced by their role.

Correct

Background

Surface

Primary

Danger

Incorrect

Blue

Dark Gray

Purple

Green

Roles remain consistent even if the actual color changes.
